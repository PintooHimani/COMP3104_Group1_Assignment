1. Institution and learning approach
George Brown College is a public postsecondary institution in Toronto, Ontario. Its experiential learning resources emphasize practical experience. A collaborative technical task helps students connect course concepts with a result they can inspect and explain.

2. Organization and accountability
A group project works better when each member understands their responsibilities. Student IDs in filenames prevent ambiguity, while branch names connect contributions with their owners. Members should agree on a review order and communicate when work is ready. This makes missing material easier to spot before submission.

3. Preparing understandable evidence
Technical work should include evidence a reader can interpret. A screenshot needs a caption identifying whose work it shows and what it demonstrates. A final report should use the actual repository link and real workflow results. These habits make a student's process easier to assess and support clear professional communication.
