Importance of DevOps in the software development lifecycle

1. Collaboration across the lifecycle
DevOps practices help people share responsibility across development and operations. During planning, a team should consider how a change will be tested, delivered, and supported. Thinking about those questions early can prevent a feature that works locally but is difficult to operate or diagnose.

2. Repeatability and review
Repeatable checks reduce variation between contributors and provide a common starting point for review. A reviewer can focus on the meaning and consequences of a change while automation handles routine structure checks. Both the checks and the documentation should improve when the team discovers a gap in its process.

3. Evidence and improvement
A successful process leaves evidence that helps a team understand a result: the relevant commit, review discussion, check output, and release information. After a problem, the team can use that evidence to improve its workflow. This assignment applies the same principle through visible member contributions, pull requests, and screenshots of actual CI runs.
